# Initial Concept
A smart English e-book reader for learners, featuring vocabulary leveling, automated word highlighting, dictionary integration, and WebDAV-based cross-device synchronization.

# Product Guide: only reading

## Vision
"only reading" is a specialized electronic book reader designed to empower English language learners by reducing the friction of reading authentic literature. By intelligently adapting to the reader's vocabulary level, it transforms a potentially frustrating experience into a seamless learning journey.

## Target Users
- **Primary:** English language learners (Students, candidates for CET-4/6, TOEFL, GRE, IELTS).
- **Secondary:** Readers who wish to maintain or expand their English proficiency through immersion in novels and essays.

## Core Goals
- **Accessibility:** Make English literature approachable by automating the "dictionary lookup" phase of reading.
- **Personalization:** Tailor the reading experience to the user's specific vocabulary level, ensuring that highlights are relevant and useful.
- **Portability:** Allow users to switch between devices (desktop, tablet, mobile) without losing their books, progress, or learning data.

## Key Features
- **Intelligent Level-Based Highlighting:** Automatically marks words that are likely unknown based on the user's selected proficiency level (CET-4 to GRE).
- **Integrated Dual Dictionary:** Offers a fast, lightweight dictionary for common words and a comprehensive large dictionary for advanced terms, both available offline once loaded.
- **Smart Vocabulary Management:** Enables users to mark words as "known" or "unknown," building a persistent personal vocabulary list.
- **WebDAV Synchronization:** Uses the user's own cloud storage to sync e-books, reading progress, and settings, ensuring data ownership and privacy.
- **Reading Statistics:** Automatically tracks reading time with inactivity detection, providing daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly insights into learning progress.
- **Multi-Format Support:** Robust support for EPUB and TXT formats with intelligent pagination and navigation.

## Success Metrics
- Reduction in manual dictionary lookups reported by users.
- Consistent growth of the user's "Known Words" database.
- Seamless transition between multiple devices during a single reading session.
